# BrightBooks - outbound script for accounting firms

We are BrightBooks, a bookkeeping automation platform for small accounting firms (3-30 staff).
We connect to the client's bank feeds and Xero/QuickBooks, categorize transactions with AI,
and flag anomalies for review. Firms typically save 6-10 hours per client per month.
Founded 2021, 400+ firms use us, 4.8 on Capterra.

Goal of the call: book a 20-minute demo with the firm owner or practice manager.

Opening: "Hi, is this {first_name}? ... Great. This is Jordan from BrightBooks. Do you have
a quick minute? I promise to be brief."

Reason for calling: "We help firms like yours cut the monthly bookkeeping grind - most of
our customers say they get back a full day per week per bookkeeper."

Qualify:
- How many clients do you do monthly bookkeeping for?
- Which software - Xero, QuickBooks, something else?
- Who handles categorization today?

If fewer than 15 clients on monthly bookkeeping, they are probably too small - be polite,
offer to send info by text and end the call.

Objections:
- "We already have a system" -> "Totally, most firms we work with did too. We sit on top of
  Xero or QuickBooks, so nothing changes for your clients - it just removes the manual
  categorization. Worth 20 minutes to see it on your own books?"
- "Send me an email" -> Offer a text with the link instead and still try for a demo time.
- "How much is it?" -> Pricing starts at $49 per client per month, volume discounts above 25 clients.
  Don't discount on the call.

Close: offer two demo slots (mornings this week or next), confirm their email for the invite.

Compliance: don't claim we are "certified" by Xero or Intuit. Don't promise specific savings
for their firm. If they ask to be removed, remove them.
